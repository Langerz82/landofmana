Privacy Policy

Land of Mana
Effective date: 14/10/2026

Welcome to Land of Mana. Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you create an account and play Land of Mana, how we use and protect it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information needed to create and operate your account:

  • User ID – the username you choose, used to identify your account and display you to other players in the game.
  • Password – used to secure and sign in to your account.
  • E-mail address – used to communicate with you about your account.
  • Online game activity – information about how you play, such as your characters, progress, items, achievements, in-game transactions, login times, and other actions you take in the game.
  • Chat messages – messages you send to other players through the in-game chat, which we collect and store on our servers.
  • IP address – the internet address of the device you use to connect to the game, which our servers log automatically each time you sign in or play.

We do not ask for any other personal information to play Land of Mana.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create, maintain, and authenticate your game account;
  • Let you sign in and keep your game progress associated with your account;
  • Save and restore your game activity so you can continue where you left off;
  • Deliver chat messages between players and keep a record of them;
  • Review game activity and chat messages to investigate reports of cheating, harassment, abuse, or other breaches of our rules;
  • Understand how the game is played so we can fix problems and improve Land of Mana;
  • Help you recover access to your account (for example, if you forget your password);
  • Send you important notices about your account, security, or changes to the game or this policy;
  • Maintain the security and integrity of the game and prevent misuse;
  • Use logged IP addresses to detect suspicious sign-ins, protect accounts from unauthorised access, and identify and block cheating, abuse, or players who have been banned.

3. How We Protect Your Information

Your password is encrypted on our servers and is never stored in plain text. We also take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your user ID and e-mail address from unauthorised access, loss, misuse, or alteration.

No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, so while we work hard to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security. We recommend using a strong, unique password for your Land of Mana account.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with any third parties. Your user ID, password, e-mail address, IP address, game activity, and chat messages are used solely to operate Land of Mana.

Please note that chat messages you send in the game are visible to the other players you send them to, and your user ID and some game activity (such as your character name, level, or rankings) may be visible to other players as part of normal gameplay. Please don't share personal information in chat that you would not want other players to see.

The only exception is where we are required to disclose information by law, such as in response to a valid court order or other legal obligation.

5. Data Retention

Your game activity, chat messages, and IP addresses are recorded in our server logs, and these logs are stored permanently.

You can delete your game account at any time. When you do, your account will be closed and you will no longer be able to sign in or access your characters and progress. However, records of your past game activity, chat messages, and IP addresses that are already stored in our logs will not be deleted and will continue to be kept permanently.

6. Your Rights and Choices

You may:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you;
  • Ask us to correct your e-mail address or other account details;
  • Change your password at any time;
  • Delete your game account (please note that game activity, chat, and IP address logs are kept permanently, as described in Section 5).

To make any of these requests, please contact us using the details below.

7. Children's Privacy

Land of Mana is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under this age. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make significant changes, we will notify you by e-mail or through a notice in the game. The "Effective date" at the top of this page shows when the policy was last updated.
